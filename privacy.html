Privacy policy

Who uses it: one household, its own devices only.

What data: the status and settings of that household's own Google Nest thermostat and doorbell (temperature, setpoints, mode, doorbell and motion events, camera stream).

Where it goes: only to a Home Assistant server running inside the same home. It is not sent to any other party, not sold, not shared, and not used for advertising or any purpose other than running the home.

Removing access: access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account or Google Home settings.
